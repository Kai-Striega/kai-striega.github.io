title = 'Polarisation and the Preferential Vote'
+++

A series of back-of-the-envelope calculations by someone who knows almost nothing about politics. How Australians
rate and rank their parties from 1993 to 2025, and what preferential voting does with those rankings: whether it
squeezes out the centre, whether it keeps out the parties most voters reject, and who wins from behind. Six posts
for the argument (an introduction, four findings and a wrap-up), and five appendices for the working.
